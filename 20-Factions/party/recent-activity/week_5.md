# Party — Week 5 Recap

In **week 5** the party [buys three Candlekeep-key books at the Pilgrim's Parchment](../activity/week_5/the-pilgrims-parchment-buy.md), [kills Vex in a warehouse ambush in Gray Harbour](../activity/week_5/kill-vex-and-loot-her-office.md), and walks out with the intel cache that breaks the city's hidden plots open. They [bring it to Alena and Mad Meggan at the Basilisk Gate camp](../activity/week_5/the-rivington-war-council.md), reach **Level 5**, and become the alliance's field instrument. Concurrent operations: [mining Eastway with the captured smokepowder while detonating the Velvet Hand](../activity/week_5/mine-eastway-and-detonate-the-velvet-hand.md); [walking out of Errinthal's Coster Hall ambush as the smiling escort of a relief caravan they then turn into a free feast](../activity/week_5/errinthals-relief-caravan-pr-coup.md); and [installing Finneas as the new Guild Boss of Rivington](../activity/week_5/install-finneas-as-guild-boss-of-rivington.md), with [Simbar](../../guild/people/simbar.md) as nominal puppet, behind which Kaelan also lands a [Baldur's Mouth investigative-journalism position from Ettvard Needle](../../baldurs-mouth/people/ettvard-needle.md) and the **Credential of Balduran**. Current leverage: see [leverage.md](../leverage/README.md).
