# Party — Week 2 Recap

In **week 2** the party [sold the captured cultist to 'Mad' Meggan for 6,000 gp](../activity/week_2/sell-the-cultist-to-mad-meggan.md) at the Basilisk Gate siege, openly defying a [Fist warrant](../../flaming-fist/activity/week_2/plan-the-unthinkable.md) and breaking with the [Chionthar Consortium](../../chionthar-consortium/activity/week_2/the-public-patrons.md). The [Guild has formally broken with them](../../guild/activity/week_2/retaliation-on-the-river.md) over their refusal to surrender the **Whispering Stone**, and [Roah Moonglow's Zhentarim](../../zhentarim/activity/week_2/consolidate-the-east-end.md) has offered to erase the warrant in exchange for Black Network service. [Bror's secret-trade with Mother Nocturne](../activity/week_2/attune-to-the-whispering-stone.md) at the Penumbra Playhouse has [embedded the Silent Shroud as an intelligence asset inside the party](../../silent-shroud/activity/week_2/amplify-the-despair.md). The party reaches **Level 4** at the end of session 9. Current leverage: see [leverage.md](../leverage/README.md).
