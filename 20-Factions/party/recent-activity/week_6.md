# Party — Week 6 Recap

In **week 6** the party reads [the *Baldur's Mouth* Siege Edition with Kaelan's *Insurance Scam* exposé folded inside](../../baldurs-mouth/activity/week_6/the-siege-edition.md), Spark proposes [the Reverse Siege strategy](../activity/week_6/the-reverse-siege-pitch.md) — reinforce the Basilisk Gate barricade and starve the Upper City — and [the party rides for New Elturel to recruit The Ember](../activity/week_6/recruit-the-ember.md). The Ember will not commit until the Takers' target on her back is removed. The party's investigation of the captured Reaper at the High Rider's Stand turns up [Singa's cell on Dusthawk Hill](../../takers-of-the-tithe/people/singa.md) and a **mole in the Hellrider supply line**; [Alfira is abducted mid-interrogation](../../../50-Sessions/session_25/player_summary.md), and [the party storms the Hill](../activity/week_6/storm-dusthawk-hill.md). Four Cairns levy theological-debt audits along the way: Kaelan and Elia refuse and absorb damage and curses, Spark accepts an Exhaustion, **Bror sells his voice to [the Skittering Beetle](../../takers-of-the-tithe/people/the-skittering-beetle.md)**. At the Counting House summit Singa's soul-link mirrors damage onto Alfira; Bror grapples Singa into a smokepowder-detonating obsidian pit; the Beetle accepts **the physical Tithe Ledger** in trade for the party's lives and lifts every curse; Kaelan and Jaredith finish Singa with *Fireball* and *Chromatic Orb*. **Singa is dead. Alfira is revived. Jaredith stays in New Elturel to oversee the Phoenix Temple construction.** The party walks Alfira to the Plowman to rest. Kaelan crosses to Heapside to find the [Baldur's Mouth print shop ransacked, Ettvard Needle missing](../activity/week_6/split-leads-after-the-counting-house.md), with a hidden note hinting Ettvard has fled to the Tumbledown crypts — *just where the party left a smoking crater the day before*. Bror, Elia, and Finn deliver Bror's secret to [Mother Nocturne](../../silent-shroud/people/mother-nocturne.md); the Night Mother demands the **physical sentient source**, not the words; the debt remains unpaid. Current leverage: see [leverage.md](../leverage/README.md). Open threads: rescue Ettvard Needle from Tumbledown; produce a sentient secret-source for Mother Nocturne; figure out who the Hellrider mole is; press on with the Reverse Siege.
