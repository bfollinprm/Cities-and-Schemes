# Party — Week 7 Recap

In **week 7** the party opens the tenday at [the Plowman](../../../30-Places/sword-coast/baldurs-gate/outer-city/rivington.md) reading the new (post-Needle) [*Baldur's Mouth*](../../baldurs-mouth/activity/week_7/the-puppet-press.md) — visibly Consortium-aligned, blaming the [Sow & Piglet massacre](../../plague-of-remembrance/activity/week_7/the-refugee-riot.md) on the Plague-of-Remembrance disguising as the Fist, and reporting [Master Phineas Droog's antidote restriction](../../wc-alchemists-apothecaries/activity/week_7/the-antidote-monopoly.md) and [K'laar's astral quarantine](../../unchained/activity/week_7/the-astral-quarantine.md) as wartime necessities. They [visit Dammon's forge](../activity/week_7/visit-dammons-forge.md): Dammon outfits Bror with **dwarven unarmed-strike gauntlets (+1 to hit)**, tests Spark's cloak with a glowing forge prong (the fire reads as *welcoming*, not painful), and reveals the cloak's clasp and tendrils are forged from **Karlach's discarded original-heart infernal iron**. They [cross the Eastway blockade to Tumbledown](../activity/week_7/cross-the-eastway-blockade-to-tumbledown.md) carrying Phineas Droog's **Guild coin** and the name of [Garrett "Grit" Fallow](../../guild/people/garrett-grit-fallow.md), a gravedigger on Nine-Fingers Keene's quiet retainer. The Sembian gate detail is preoccupied with deserter-mercenaries from [the Vintner Dry Riot](../../wc-vintners-brewers/activity/week_7/the-dry-riot.md); the party's poor disguises pass unnoticed. They reach Tumbledown by midafternoon and clock a **five-strong Gravekeeper patrol** in the next yard ([Hemlock's Cliffside Consecration](../../gravekeepers/activity/week_7/the-cliffside-consecration.md) is operationally on). What the party does not know yet: [the Takers' Hunter-Killer cell from the Collector is shadowing the Plowman](../../takers-of-the-tithe/activity/week_7/the-auditors-revenge.md) and has logged Bror, Elia, and Finn's departure for Tumbledown. Current leverage: see [leverage.md](../leverage/README.md). Open threads: the gravedigger patrol decision; the descent into the Tumbledown crypts to find Ettvard Needle alive; the still-unpaid sentient-source debt to Mother Nocturne; the Reverse Siege.
