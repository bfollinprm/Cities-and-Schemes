# Party — Week 3 Recap

In **week 3** the party [evades the Fist warrant](../activity/week_3/evade-the-fist-warrant.md) at the Weary Plowman with [Lena's](../../../30-Places/sword-coast/baldurs-gate/outer-city/people/lena.md) help and walks the [Circus of the Last Days investigation](../activity/week_3/infiltrate-the-circus-of-the-last-days.md) for [the Guild](../../guild/activity/week_3/recover-the-asset.md). Inside, **Elia and Bror each accept fey bargains with [the mirror-tent proprietor](../../carnival-of-whispers/people/mirror-tent-proprietor.md) — one true secret each, owed at a later date.** They [force the Feywild portal](../activity/week_3/force-the-feywild-portal.md), confront [Lucretius](../../carnival-of-whispers/people/lucretius.md) inside *The Ephemera Collection*, and [win Lyra Sunstone's memory back](../activity/week_3/win-lyras-memory-from-lucretius.md) by answering three riddles. Lucretius honors the bargain and [activates Elia's shard](../activity/week_3/unlock-the-shard-of-the-first-scribe.md) — Elia and Bror share a cosmic vision of a forgotten **God of Naming** that severed the very piece of itself Elia now carries. The shard reshapes into the **Shard of the First Scribe**. The party returns to find **two weeks have passed** on the Material Plane; the Fist warrant has cooled.
