# Followers Of The Phoenix — Methods

- **Public sermons** at the Phoenix Gate stage; rhetoric that turns protests into rallies.
- **The [Wyrm's Crossing](../../30-Places/sword-coast/baldurs-gate/outer-city/wyrms-crossing.md) protest** alongside the [Hellriders of New Elturel](../hellriders-of-new-elturel/summary.md), de-escalated by Jaredith's song.
- **The Foundation Stone** — Temple of the Phoenix construction at Phoenix Gate, with volunteer stone and labor despite the blockade. The [Ember's first formal sermon at the temple ground (week 3)](activity/week_3/the-first-sermon-at-the-phoenix-gate.md), with Jaredith standing visibly at her side, makes the Phoenix–Hellrider alignment a public fact and turns the construction site into the Outer City's primary spiritual venue. After [the Survivor's Sermon (week 5)](activity/week_5/the-survivors-sermon.md) — preached over the Reaper assassin's blade as a prop, attendance triples — **construction on the permanent stone foundation begins in earnest**, Mikkel breaking ground on a foundation set deep into the Phoenix Gate hardpan. In [week 7's Saint's Respite](activity/week_7/the-saints-respite.md), with Alfira convalescing at the Plowman after Dusthawk Hill and Jaredith presiding daily with a lute on his back, **the temple's stone footprint is fully laid by the seventh day**. The faction has consolidated from a street-level cult into a *physical institution with permanent roots in Baldur's Gate* — donations now arrive from refugees who have never set foot in the camp.
- **Martyrdom narrative** — surviving an assassination by the [Takers of the Tithe](../takers-of-the-tithe/summary.md) was repurposed into a sermon: the city's rot fears their light.
- **Soft power** — empathy, art, and faith over force; the crowd is "literally ready to start fires" if The Ember falls.
