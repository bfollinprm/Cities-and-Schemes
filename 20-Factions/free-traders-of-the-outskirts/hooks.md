# Free Traders Of The Outskirts — Hooks and Relationships

**Enemies.** Constant conflict with the [Flaming Fist](../flaming-fist/summary.md) over gate tolls — Meggan has been beaten in person, and the Free Traders responded with blockades. Resist [The Guild](../guild/summary.md)'s protection rackets. Targeted by the [Chionthar Consortium](../chionthar-consortium/summary.md), which has used its Fist influence to **impound Meggan's vessel** under the Harbor Dredging Levy.

**Allies.** Natural allies of the [Hellriders of New Elturel](../hellriders-of-new-elturel/summary.md). Quietly reinforced by the [Circle of the Inner Grove](../circle-of-the-inner-grove/summary.md), which is warding the Basilisk Gate blockade. The [Worshipful Company of Fishmongers](../wc-fishmongers/summary.md) has begun selling salted fish at cost to the blockaders. The [Worshipful Company of Cartographers & Surveyors](../wc-cartographers-surveyors/summary.md) is selling tactical maps to both sides.

**Hooks.** Meggan's pact has matured into a sustained **blockade of [Wyrm's Crossing](../../30-Places/sword-coast/baldurs-gate/outer-city/wyrms-crossing.md)** and **Basilisk Gate**. The Basilisk blockade was violently broken by [Blaze Liara Portyr](../flaming-fist/summary.md) — but the fallout has only enraged the [Outer City](../../30-Places/sword-coast/baldurs-gate/outer-city/README.md) further. Meggan extracted a **public confession** from a captive [Plague of Remembrance](../plague-of-remembrance/summary.md) cultist naming several patriar families as backers; *Baldur's Mouth* has refused to print her unsubstantiated claims under patriar pressure. The party may have benefited from a large, untraceable payment funneled through Meggan via shell accounts that the [WC Goldsmiths](../wc-goldsmiths-jewelers/summary.md) cannot unwind.

**Defensive militia posture (week 5).** In response to [the Archduke's three-day ultimatum](../council-of-four/activity/week_5/issue-the-ultimatum.md), Meggan [organizes her caravan crews into a fortified inner barricade with makeshift weapons](activity/week_5/heed-the-ultimatum.md) — wagons staggered behind the original blockade, smiths beating trade-iron into pike-heads, stall-keepers learning to load crossbows. **The defensive-militia posture is now a real military stance.** [High Watcher Alena's formal alliance offer](../hellriders-of-new-elturel/activity/week_5/the-elturgard-alliance.md) arrives the same evening and is signed on a barrel-head — Hellrider regulars now drill behind the inner barricade.
