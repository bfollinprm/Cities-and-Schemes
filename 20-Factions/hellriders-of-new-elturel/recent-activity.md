# Hellriders — Recent Activity

Week-by-week prose recap. Authoritative per-action canon lives in [`activity/`](activity/).

In **week 2** Alena broke the cultist and used the intelligence to point the party at the Plague-of-Remembrance agent **Zora** in New Elturel; the [party exposed Zora at the Phoenix Pits](../party/activity/week_2/expose-zora-at-the-phoenix-pits.md) and [survived her watchtower ambush](../party/activity/week_2/survive-the-watchtower-ambush.md), forcing her extraction from the city. Alena nearly died at the watchtower. She used the protest's peaceful end and the goodwill from Jaredith's deconversion to [open formal citizenship negotiations with Lyra Sunstone and the Council](activity/week_2/the-interrogation.md) — but the same week, [the Ember and Jaredith ended the Wyrm's Crossing protest in song](../party/activity/week_2/witness-jaredith-end-the-protest.md) and the **Followers of the Phoenix have eclipsed Alena's disciplined Hellriders as the dominant faith movement in New Elturel.**

In **week 3** Alena [walks out of the citizenship negotiations](activity/week_3/the-negotiations-price.md) when the Council's terms required Hellrider abandonment of [the Free Trader siege](../free-traders-of-the-outskirts/activity/week_3/press-the-advantage.md) and a public condemnation of Mad Meggan. Negotiations collapse. The Hellriders now publicly stand with Meggan against the Council; the formal Hellrider–Free Trader military alliance becomes inevitable once [the Archduke's private mercenaries arrive](../western-gate-trading-company/activity/week_3/the-private-option.md). [Lyra Sunstone](people/lyra-sunstone.md), captured by the Carnival of Whispers and stripped of her memories, is rescued and restored by the party in a Feywild bargain — Alena recovers a senior officer who now owes the party a personal debt.

In **week 4** Alena reads [the Archduke's mercenaries fortifying inside the Basilisk Gate](../council-of-four/activity/week_4/the-mercenary-deployment.md) as the inevitable attack she warned the Council was coming and [issues a formal call to arms](activity/week_4/the-call-to-arms.md) — refugees organized into militia brigades, Jaredith drilling them dawn and dusk, [Lyra Sunstone restored and signing requisitions](people/lyra-sunstone.md). The same week, [the Takers of the Tithe's Reaper attempt on the Ember at the Phoenix Gate fails](../takers-of-the-tithe/activity/week_4/the-reapers-gambit.md) and the militia takes the assassin into custody alive. The Hellrider militia and the Phoenix congregation are now visibly the same crowd. **Concealed risk:** [Lady Vhage of the Plague of Remembrance has turned a disillusioned Hellrider officer bitter over the failed citizenship negotiations](../plague-of-remembrance/activity/week_4/the-rot-from-within.md); the cell now has an ear inside Alena's command tent.

In **week 5** Alena [signs a formal military alliance with 'Mad' Meggan against the Archduke](activity/week_5/the-elturgard-alliance.md) within hours of [the Archduke's three-day ultimatum](../council-of-four/activity/week_5/issue-the-ultimatum.md): a full Hellrider century deploys behind the Free Trader inner barricade, Jaredith drilling the joint force twice daily. **The militia of New Elturel and the Free Traders of the Outskirts are now one army.** Almost simultaneously, [a Plague of Remembrance courtesan-agent attempts to drug a senior Hellrider officer for the alliance dispositions](../plague-of-remembrance/activity/week_5/the-serpents-kiss.md) — the attempt fails when the officer notices a wax-seal mismatch, but Alena now knows the cult has agents inside her command tent (her *pre-existing* turned officer remains undetected).

In **week 6** the Hellriders [absorb the Council's warning-shot volley behind a tested shield wall at the Basilisk Gate](activity/week_6/the-shield-wall.md) without a ripple — the barricade is now a *front*, not a line. The same week, [a corrupt cook on the Hellrider supply chain poisons the captured Reaper](../takers-of-the-tithe/activity/week_6/the-prison-silence.md) mid-confession to the party at the High Rider's Stand. **There is now a confirmed mole in Alena's logistics**, separate from the turned officer in her command tent. Both are unidentified. The Hellrider stockade has hosted [Eric, an imprisoned minor patriarch](../peerage-of-blood/people/eric.md) for weeks; Alfira herself was abducted from the camp during the party's interrogation. Alena, currently at the Basilisk Gate, returns at week's end to a camp that is openly nervous about its own kitchen.
