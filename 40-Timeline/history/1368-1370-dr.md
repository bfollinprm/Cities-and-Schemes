## 1368–1370 DR — Bhaalspawn era and the Flaming Fist

- **1368 DR — Iron Crisis (BG I).** Sarevok Anchev tries to spark war between Baldur's Gate and Amn for godhood; stopped by Abdel Adrian.
- **1369 DR — Bhaalspawn Crisis (BG II / Throne of Bhaal).** Abdel Adrian forsakes the Throne of Bhaal and remains mortal; becomes a celebrated Sword Coast hero.
- **1370 DR — Founding of the Flaming Fist.** Grand Duke Eltan charters the mercenary company; soon hired as Baldur's Gate's primary police and military force.
