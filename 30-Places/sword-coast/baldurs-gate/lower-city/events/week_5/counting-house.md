## Counting House

- **Peerage of Coin's Emergency Tax dies in the workshops.** [Guild](../../../../../../20-Factions/guild/summary.md) wardens physically block tax collectors out of the Lower City workshops; the levy fails, the chamber is humiliated, and the city now knows the Peerage of Coin cannot enforce its own laws without the Guild's permission. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/peerage-of-coin/activity/week_5/the-emergency-tax.md).
- **Goldsmiths abandon the party-bounty search.** A week of paid bribes for fabricated leads ends in [Master Goldhand's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-goldsmiths-jewelers/people/theronius-goldhand.md) public outburst; the search is called off; key banking contacts are alienated. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-goldsmiths-jewelers/activity/week_5/chasing-ghosts.md).
- **Zhentarim infiltrate the Counting House.** [Roah Moonglow's](../../../../../../20-Factions/zhentarim/people/roah-moonglow.md) post-Velvet-Hand pivot — agents inside the city's private bank manipulating ledgers and forging documents to disrupt Guild-front legitimate businesses. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/zhentarim/activity/week_5/the-financial-front.md).
