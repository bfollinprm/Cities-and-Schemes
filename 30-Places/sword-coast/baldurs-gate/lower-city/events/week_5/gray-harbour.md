## Gray Harbour

- **A Guild Lieutenant is murdered in a Dock Ward warehouse.** [Vex](../../../../../../20-Factions/guild/people/vex.md) springs a pre-planned ambush on the party in a non-descript warehouse; the trap fails; Vex dies and the party walks out with [her intel cache](../../../../../../20-Factions/party/activity/week_5/kill-vex-and-loot-her-office.md). One archer escapes, [Simbar](../../../../../../20-Factions/guild/people/simbar.md), and is captured later that night. The body is hidden in saffron crates marked with the Amnian Kontor seal — confirmation of the [Farlin skim](../../../../../../20-Factions/guild/people/farlin.md). See [Guild — Prepare the Retaliation](../../../../../../20-Factions/guild/activity/week_5/prepare-the-retaliation.md).
- **Amnian Coin-Swords seize two Consortium river barges at the docks.** Retaliation for [last week's Consortium "warning"](../../../../../../20-Factions/chionthar-consortium/activity/week_4/a-warning-in-the-warehouse.md): [Consul Athar](../../../../../../20-Factions/amnian-kontor/people/vorlamin-athar.md) calls in a major debt and impounds two of the Consortium's finest barges in a public, humiliating action. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/amnian-kontor/activity/week_5/retaliatory-asset-seizure.md).
- **Shipwrights' monopoly on dock repairs cracks.** Independent crews unite under [Wharf Rat](../../../../../../20-Factions/wharf-rats/summary.md) protection and fight back; [Master Tallowshand's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-shipwrights-calkers/people/helma-tallowshand.md) Wardens are publicly thrown into the harbor. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-shipwrights-calkers/activity/week_5/profiting-from-chaos.md).
- **Wharf Rats hit by a Zhent traitor.** A Zhentarim hit squad locates [One-Eyed Jack's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wharf-rats/people/one-eyed-jack.md) safehouse via a turncoat in his own crew; the Rats survive only by fighting a desperate defensive battle. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wharf-rats/activity/week_5/licking-their-wounds.md).
- **Fishmongers' poisoner-bounty produces nothing but lies.** [Marga Saltwind's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-fishmongers/people/marga-saltwind.md) reward draws hundreds of false tips; her enforcers brawl with innocent sailors; the guild's reputation worsens. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-fishmongers/activity/week_5/find-the-poisoner.md).
- **Lantaner foundry scavenge into the Steel Watch Foundry.** [Glim-Nimblewhiz's](../../../../../../20-Factions/lantaner-concession/people/glim-nimblewhiz.md) team mounts a clandestine alloy-recovery expedition into the defunct foundry to rebuild the prototype after [the explosion](../../../../../../20-Factions/lantaner-concession/activity/week_4/salvage-operation.md). See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/lantaner-concession/activity/week_5/the-foundry-scavenge.md).
