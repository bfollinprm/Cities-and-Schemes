## Heapside

- **Unveiled break their captured comrades out of Heapside Prison.** [Xylia](../../../../../../20-Factions/unveiled/people/xylia.md) uses forgotten Undercity tunnels to free the operatives [captured at the cellar-grate ambush](../../../../../../20-Factions/unveiled/activity/week_4/the-prison-break.md) — a survival operation that buys the cell another week. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/unveiled/activity/week_5/the-great-escape.md).
- **Society of Baldurian Integrity's prison visit.** [Calliope](../../../../../../20-Factions/society-of-baldurian-integrity/people/calliope.md), disguised as a relative, slips a coded "drunken-merchant vandalism" cover story to the captured nativists at Heapside Prison. The Society's existence remains protected. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/society-of-baldurian-integrity/activity/week_5/the-prison-visit.md).
- **Hands of the Absolute's psychic miasma backfires in a Heapside attic.** [The Voice in the Choir's](../../../../../../20-Factions/hands-of-the-absolute/people/the-voice.md) ritual to harvest the city's fear knocks her unconscious and burns out the cell's resonance chamber. The cult is **psychically blinded**. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/hands-of-the-absolute/activity/week_5/the-psychic-miasma.md).
- **Kaelan secures a Baldur's Mouth investigative-journalism position.** [Ettvard Needle](../../../../../../20-Factions/baldurs-mouth/people/ettvard-needle.md) hands Kaelan the **Credential of Balduran** signet ring; information dropboxes set up at the print shop and at the Plowman. See [Install Finneas as Guild boss of Rivington (party)](../../../../../../20-Factions/party/activity/week_5/install-finneas-as-guild-boss-of-rivington.md).
