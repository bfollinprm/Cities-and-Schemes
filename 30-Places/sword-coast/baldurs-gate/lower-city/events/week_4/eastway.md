## Eastway

- **Mercenary barracks stood up in the requisitioned WGTC warehouse.** [Archduke Lyra Silvershield](../../../../../../20-Factions/western-gate-trading-company/people/lyra-silvershield.md) [unilaterally deploys her private mercenary company](../../../../../../20-Factions/council-of-four/activity/week_4/the-mercenary-deployment.md); the warehouse is converted to barracks, mess, and command tent in days. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/western-gate-trading-company/activity/week_4/the-barracks-requisition.md).
- **City-Sanctioned Peacekeepers contract filed.** [Bursar Korgan Ironhand](../../../../../../20-Factions/sembian-kontor/people/korgan-ironhand.md) walks the Sembian Kontor's instrument through the bureaucracy; the mercenaries are now an arm of municipal authority on paper. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/sembian-kontor/activity/week_4/legitimizing-the-contract.md).
- **Free Trader fortification effort halted by mercenary shield-wall.** ['Mad' Meggan's](../../../../../../20-Factions/free-traders-of-the-outskirts/people/mad-meggan.md) order to dig ditches and sharpen stakes is broken in front of the city by a single mercenary march. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/free-traders-of-the-outskirts/activity/week_4/digging-in.md).
- **Harper back-channel meeting at the Singing Lute leaked.** The [Nightingale's](../../../../../../20-Factions/harpers/people/the-nightingale.md) and Randal Evenwood's compromise meeting between moderate patriars and Free Trader / Hellrider voices is exposed in a rival pamphlet; trust evaporates; the compromise dies. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/harpers/activity/week_4/the-back-channel-bargain.md).
- **Society of Baldurian Integrity slogan-paint raid breaks down.** Mercenary sentries spot [Calliope's](../../../../../../20-Factions/society-of-baldurian-integrity/people/calliope.md) followers from rooftop overwatch; arrests, sworn statements, the Society's first Watch file. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/society-of-baldurian-integrity/activity/week_4/the-writing-on-the-wall.md).
- **Solidarity-stew kitchen sabotaged into a food-poisoning incident.** [Matron Marga Saltwind's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-fishmongers/people/marga-saltwind.md) Free Trader goodwill kitchen at the Basilisk Gate is sabotaged — most plausibly by [Plague-of-Remembrance hands](../../../../../../20-Factions/plague-of-remembrance/activity/week_4/the-rot-from-within.md); dozens of blockaders sickened, kitchen shut down by dusk. See [Fishmongers activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-fishmongers/activity/week_4/the-solidarity-stew.md).
- **Vintners pivot allegiance to the Eastway barracks.** [Master Belba Quickfoot](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-vintners-brewers/people/belba-quickfoot.md), publicly humiliated by Durinbold last week, delivers a "hospitality gift" of rare spirits and wines to the mercenary commander; the Vintners become the informal house brewer. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-vintners-brewers/activity/week_4/a-new-patron.md).
- **Cartographers' Guild double-sells Basilisk Gate tactical surveys.** [Master Vancroft](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-cartographers-surveyors/people/elias-vancroft.md) profits from both sides of the impending conflict, with neither buyer aware. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-cartographers-surveyors/activity/week_4/playing-both-sides.md).
- **Chandlers' lamplighter informant network compromised.** A Zhentarim agent turns one of [Anya Brightwood's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-chandlers-lamplighters/people/anya-brightwood.md) informants; the Black Network now feeds [Vex](../../../../../../20-Factions/guild/people/vex.md) deliberately false intelligence. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-chandlers-lamplighters/activity/week_4/eyes-in-the-dark.md).
