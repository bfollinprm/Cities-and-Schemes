## Gray Harbour

- **Consortium "warning" goes loud.** [Factor Errinthal's](../../../../../../20-Factions/chionthar-consortium/people/factor-errinthal.md) sloppy crate-drop staging is witnessed by a Watch patrol; perpetrators arrested for destruction of property; the trade dispute escalates into a *criminal matter*. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/chionthar-consortium/activity/week_4/a-warning-in-the-warehouse.md).
- **Shipwrights' rigged crane "malfunction" cracks the Consortium dock.** [Master Tallowshand's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-shipwrights-calkers/people/helma-tallowshand.md) loosened load-pin sends a pallet through the deck; two longshoremen injured, warehouse front cracked, lost shipments. Errinthal traces it to her but cannot prove it without giving up his enforcers. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wc-shipwrights-calkers/activity/week_4/a-crane-malfunction.md).
- **Gilded Vein grain-warehouse sabotage exposes Thorne.** A Vein agent leaves an alchemical compound that traces back to [Valerius Thorne's](../../../../../../20-Factions/gilded-vein/people/valerius-thorne.md) underworld signature; both Amn and the Consortium learn they have been *played*. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/gilded-vein/activity/week_4/poisoning-the-well.md).
- **Lantaner salvage run on the Steel Watch Foundry mauled by surviving constructs.** [Glim-Nimblewhiz's](../../../../../../20-Factions/lantaner-concession/people/glim-nimblewhiz.md) crew retreats empty-handed; **functioning Steel Watchers are still inside the foundry**, now logged as a city-scale concern. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/lantaner-concession/activity/week_4/salvage-operation.md).
- **Wharf Rats' guerilla war crushed.** [One-Eyed Jack's](../../../../../../20-Factions/wharf-rats/people/one-eyed-jack.md) ambushes succeed briefly; the Zhent response is professionally brutal; Wharf Rat corpses are made a public example on the pier. See [activity](../../../../../../20-Factions/wharf-rats/activity/week_4/the-pier-side-war.md).
